Straight Answers

What it doesn't do

MrWiseMax is deliberately small. It won't connect to your bank, import transactions, categorise your spending, run investment simulations, or give you a score out of a hundred. It does one thing: it tells you whether the money you have covers the money you've already committed to — and when it stops.

It's a calculator over figures you type, not financial advice. Keep your balances current and it stays useful.
